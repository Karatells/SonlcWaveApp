SonicWave Studio is an open-source, privacy-first Hi-Res audio player for Navidrome, OpenSubsonic, Jellyfin, and local audio folders.

Features:
* Lossless & Hi-Res streaming (FLAC, ALAC, OPUS, M4A, MP3)
* Offline vault with pinned downloads and automatic cache management
* 10-band graphic equalizer, Bass Boost, Virtualizer, and ReplayGain
* Animated vinyl turntable player with synced LRC lyrics and waveform seekbar
* Dual-pane landscape studio console and navigation rail
* Multi-server profiles with LAN/WAN fallback and QR config sharing
* Zero telemetry, no ads, no proprietary tracking
